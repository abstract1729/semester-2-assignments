For homework 4 you will implement a simple segmentation algorithm to 
find large or dominant 3D planes in point clouds. You should show your
results for two datasets, i.e. the sparse and dense 3D reconstructions of
the main building using COLMAP.

You can implement any method of your choice. One possibility is to use a
modification of the RANSAC idea. A key issue to think about it is the
very large number of points you have to deal with in the aforementioned
datasets. You should think of how you can find planes in subsets of
points and then use the result to develop the final answer.

In your submission briefly sketch your scheme, key implementational details and your 
findings/observations
